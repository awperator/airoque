Service 05

APQP Documentation

New product introduction generates a documentation package with dependencies in every direction. AiroQue builds and coordinates that package, aligned with applicable aerospace quality and APQP requirements and with your customer's deliverable list.

1.0 Scope

Documentation and implementation support across the launch

APQP puts a sequence around introducing a product: understand the requirements, plan the process, analyze the risk, establish controls, prove the process can hold them, and release to production with evidence. Each phase produces documents the next phase depends on, which is why launches fail on coordination more often than on capability.

AiroQue produces those deliverables and keeps them consistent with each other. Where you have an internal program manager running the launch, AiroQue works to their plan. Where you do not, AiroQue can maintain the deliverable list, flag what is late, and tell you which missing item is blocking three others.

The dependency that causes most rework

Process Flow, PFMEA and Control Plan are frequently produced in parallel by different people under schedule pressure, then reconciled at the end — which is when the reconciliation turns out to be a rewrite. Built as a connected set, the sequence costs less and survives review.

AiroQue provides documentation and implementation support. AiroQue does not certify organizations to AS9100 and does not perform third-party certification.

For programs with many parts and a fixed submission date, AiroQue can maintain the deliverable matrix — what is required per part number, who owns it, what revision it is at, and what is outstanding.

3.0 Tailoring

Every customer's APQP looks different. That is the normal case.

Phase gates, deliverable names, submission formats and approval routes vary by customer and often by program within the same customer.

Aligned to the requirement you were given

AiroQue works from the requirement documents and deliverable lists you supply. Where your customer mandates specific forms, those forms are used from the start rather than reformatted at the end.

Phase and gate naming

Documentation is organized and labelled to your customer's phase structure and gate terminology, so submissions map to what the reviewer expects to receive.

Deliverable list

Scope is set against the customer's required deliverables for the program — not a standard package. Items you already have are excluded from the quote.

Forms and submission structure

Customer-mandated forms, cover sheets, indexes and approval blocks are used as issued. Where no format is specified, AiroQue proposes one and you approve it before work proceeds.

Part-family handling

Family-level documentation with part-specific supplements, or fully independent packages per part number, depending on what your customer will accept.

Level of involvement

Full package ownership through the launch, defined deliverables only, or a review-and-correct role over documentation your own team produces.

Program cadence

Status reporting and review points set to your program's rhythm — weekly during a compressed launch, or at gates for a longer program.

FAQ

Common questions

What is included in an APQP documentation package?

A coordinated set typically covering Process Flow, PFMEA, Control Plan and supporting production-readiness documentation, structured to AS9145 expectations and your customer's format.

Is this a full PPAP package?

AiroQue builds the quality-planning documents that sit inside aerospace APQP and PPAP packages. Element lists vary by customer; scope is confirmed before work starts.

Who is this for?

AS9100 manufacturers and their Tier 1–3 suppliers who need planning documents for a new part, a process change or an upcoming customer review.
