Monthly support

Outsourced aerospace quality engineering, by the month

A reserved block of aerospace quality engineering capacity each month, for the revisions, change reviews and APQP deliverables that pile up between full-time hires. Starting at $2,500 per month.

Request a confidential project reviewSee starting rates

What a month of capacity covers

Monthly support is used for the same documentation AiroQue delivers as projects: Control Plan and PFMEA development and revision, engineering change impact reviews, inspection plans, work instructions and APQP deliverables. Everything is written in your customer's format and terminology.

What stays with your team

MRB decisions, product sign-offs, customer contact and ownership of your AS9100 quality system stay with your organization. AiroQue writes and revises the documents; your people approve and release them.

How work is requested and tracked

Requests come in through the project-review form or by email to customer.support@airoque.com. Each request names the document, the revision and the date it is needed.

Project or monthly: when each makes sense

A fixed-price project fits a defined deliverable, such as one new Control Plan or one alignment review. Monthly support fits a steady stream of revisions and changes, where scoping each one separately would slow the work down. For the hire-versus-outsource question, read whether to outsource a Control Plan or hire a quality engineer.

Starting rate and what scales it

Monthly quality engineering support starts at $2,500 per month. The rate scales with the capacity reserved and the scope of the documents involved, and is confirmed after a scope review. See all starting rates.

AiroQue is based in Simi Valley, CA. Support is delivered remotely across the United States, with on-site support available in Ventura County, Los Angeles County and the San Fernando Valley.

Need quality engineering capacity?

Send the scope and the kind of work you expect each month. AiroQue will respond with a monthly rate and a start date, and can issue an NDA first.
