What drives the final price

Two Control Plans for two parts are rarely the same size of job. Scope is reviewed before a fixed price is issued, based on:

  • Part complexity and configuration
  • Number of characteristics to be controlled
  • Number and type of manufacturing operations
  • Special processes and outside processing
  • Customer-specific formats and requirements
  • Condition of any existing documentation supplied
  • Volume of documents in the package
  • Required turnaround time

How engagements are structured

Most work is quoted as a fixed-price project. Manufacturers with recurring needs use a monthly agreement instead, which is generally more economical than quoting each revision separately.

Services are not purchased through this website. These are professional consulting services, and every project is reviewed before final pricing is issued. Requesting a quote costs nothing and commits you to nothing.

FAQ

Common questions

Are the published rates fixed quotes?

They are starting rates. Final price depends on part complexity, characteristic count, operations, customer-specific requirements and turnaround time. You receive a fixed price after scope review.

Do you offer monthly quality engineering support?

Yes. Manufacturers who need ongoing capacity rather than a single deliverable can engage AiroQue for a defined number of hours each month.

Is an NDA available before we send drawings?

Yes. Project information is treated as confidential and an NDA can be issued before any technical data is exchanged.
