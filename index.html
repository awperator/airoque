4.0 Tailoring

No two customers receive the same document.

Format, column structure, classification symbols, numbering conventions, reading level and terminology are set by your customer requirements and your plant — not by an AiroQue house template.

A Control Plan for a machined detail submitted to one prime looks nothing like a pass-through inspection plan for a distributed assembly. Both are Control Plans. Both are correct. The difference is who reads them and what they are required to prove.

Compare two example Control Plans

Their template

Customer-mandated forms are used from the start rather than reformatted at the end of the job.

Their definitions

Special-characteristic symbols, rating scales and designation rules are applied as your requirements define them.

Your numbering

Operation, characteristic and document numbering match your router, records and document control.

Your vocabulary

Cells, tools, stations and forms are named the way your people already name them.

7.0 Credibility

Built around aerospace quality principles

Services are developed with consideration of applicable aerospace quality standards, customer requirements, engineering requirements and contractual requirements.

Frameworks and concepts applied
AS9100Aviation, space and defense QMS requirementsDocumentation, process control, risk and change-management support
AS9145Aerospace APQP and PPAPPlanning phases, Process Flow, PFMEA and Control Plan deliverables
AS9103Variation management of key characteristicsKey-characteristic identification, monitoring and capability considerations
APQPAdvanced product quality planningNew product introduction and production-readiness documentation
PFMEAProcess failure mode and effects analysisStructured manufacturing process risk analysis and revision

AiroQue develops documentation and provides implementation support. AiroQue does not perform third-party certification and does not certify organizations to AS9100.
